# REVEN

AI Revenue Retention & Recovery Agent.

REVEN identifies why customer revenue is at risk and chooses the intervention that maximizes expected future net revenue, rather than simply maximizing today's payment recovery.

## Core loop

Detect → Diagnose → Predict → Decide → Authorize → Act → Measure → Learn

## Core feature

**Revenue Rescue Autopilot** — recommend and, when authorized, execute the action with the highest expected net revenue preserved.

## Possible actions

Retry · Update Payment · Billing Change · Payment Plan · Downgrade · Pause · Incentive · Escalate · Do Nothing

## Primary metric

**Expected Net Revenue Preserved**

## Safety

AI recommends → deterministic Policy Engine authorizes.
